# Privacy

W3C Companion doesn’t collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- It reads the address and content of the page in the current tab, in the browser, to recognize W3C specs, W3C
  repositories, and w3.org group pages. That information stays in the browser.
- To show information about a spec or a group, it fetches public data from api.w3.org, www.w3.org, w3c.github.io,
  w3c-oss.github.io, and respec.org. Those requests carry the name of the spec, group, or term being looked up, and
  nothing about the user.
- It stores a cache of that public data, and one setting (whether to show definition usage in pages), in the
  browser’s extension storage.
- It has no analytics and no tracking.
